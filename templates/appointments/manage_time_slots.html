How Time Slots Work

  • ✓ Each time slot has a maximum patient limit (1-50 patients)
  • ✓ Patients can see live availability (booked vs available spots)
  • ✓ When a slot reaches its limit, it becomes automatically unavailable
  • ✓ You can add multiple slots for the same day (e.g., 10am-12pm and 4pm-6pm)
  • ✓ Deleted slots won't affect existing appointments

Example

If you set 10 patients for the 2:00 PM - 3:00 PM slot:
• First 10 patients can book this slot
• 11th patient will see "Fully Booked" and cannot book
• Perfect for managing your workload efficiently!
